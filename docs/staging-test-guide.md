# Sweet Rascal staging test guide

Use a staging database, one Owner account, one Staff account, two devices, a phone for Google Contacts and WhatsApp, and the restaurant’s A4 printer. Record the device, role, order or bill number, and what happened for any failure.

1. **Navigation and branding.** Check desktop, a short desktop window, tablet and phone. The desktop header and logo block should line up. Scroll the sidebar with the wheel and use Tab to reach Settings even though the scrollbar is hidden. Check the mobile bottom bar and More. Confirm the two hearts appear on sign-in, the desktop sidebar, the mobile header, the Chrome tab, the installed app icon, and the colour and monochrome invoice. An already installed app may need reinstalling to show its new icon.
2. **Settings and profile.** Open Settings as Owner and Staff. Both should see Install app on an eligible browser and Sign out when connected. Verify Staff cannot edit restaurant details, GST, discount limits or Google Contacts settings. Open My profile from the header, save a display name, refresh, and confirm it persists. Sign out from Settings, use Back, and check that protected data still requires sign-in. Try an unsupported install browser and verify the instructions are useful.
3. **Startup and network.** Open an old `/#orders` bookmark and a new `/orders` URL. Reload on a slow connection. Both hearts should finish their current drawing before the workspace appears. Try reduced motion. Disconnect, confirm an offline message, reconnect, and verify the app recovers and previously saved records are still there. For offline operation during the pilot, use the restaurant’s agreed manual fallback.
4. **Staff access and privacy.** Sign in as Owner and Staff on separate devices. Staff should be able to serve tables, bill customers and add a dish in **Food & pricing** using an existing category. Check that the dish appears when taking an order and that Staff can't edit its saved price or add categories. Try opening Reports, staff administration, Owner settings actions, refunds, and an older invoice directly as Staff. Check that access follows the role. Disable a test Staff account and confirm its active session loses access.
5. **Menu and pricing.** Add a category and dish, rename the category, change the price, and mark a dish sold out. Check that new orders use the new price, existing lines retain their saved price, and sold-out dishes cannot be added again. Try duplicate names, invalid quantities, a special dish, a dish note and a price override with and without a reason.
6. **Tables, drafts and service stages.** Add a table, start a dine-in order, save and reopen a draft, assign a waiter, move tables, mark food served, and send it to Billing. Try two devices claiming the same table, an occupied destination, and a party larger than the table's capacity. Confirm the table stays occupied after bill issuance and frees only when departure is recorded. Check the unpaid-departure exception separately.
7. **Phone pre-orders and parcels.** Create a pre-order with guest details and an advance. Assign its table later and confirm arrival. Create a parcel without a table and test it with an optional reference table. Check collection after payment. Try cancelling an unpaid draft, then a pre-order with an advance. For the latter, record the current blocker as pending work and use a separate test record.
8. **Customers and contacts.** Search by name and phone, then create and reopen a customer with a contact note and companion. Try a shared phone number and invalid input. Verify private owner notes stay private. Open a customer's bill history and make sure a different customer with the same name is excluded. Connect the staging Google account, use a test contact you control, verify the user-facing sync status, and confirm the contact reaches the phone. Test disconnected Google access and retries.
9. **Checkout.** Apply a fixed discount and a percentage discount. Test Staff's approval limit and Owner approval. Change a line price after approving a discount, then review it again. Change GST in Settings and compare new orders with saved orders and issued bills. Apply an advance, split the balance across Cash, UPI and Card, record a voluntary tip, and test using the excess as a tip. Check zero balance, invalid amounts, duplicate clicks, partial payment, correcting a payment, and refreshing before bill issuance.
10. **Billing and printer.** Issue a bill after the balance reaches zero. Retry issuance and check the invoice number stays the same. Preview and download a short and multi-page A4 bill in colour and black and white. Check paper size, repeated item headings, totals, legible lines and the two-heart mark on actual printouts. Test reprinting and opening the correct WhatsApp chat with an authorized test number, then attach the PDF manually.
11. **Refunds and sales.** Record a partial refund against an issued test bill, then refund the remainder. Confirm refund limits, duplicate submission protection, permission checks and preservation of the original invoice. For a bill issued yesterday and refunded today, check the refund appears today and the original sale stays on its issue date.
12. **Reports and device updates.** Compare Overview and Reports for the same dates. Check sales, GST, tips and refunds separately. Test history search, filters, sorting, pagination and CSV. Open a bill from page two and return to the same results. Save an order on one device and confirm the other sees it without refreshing. Edit it on both devices and verify the stale save is rejected and unsaved text stays visible.

## Next integration phases

1. **Run the staging service test.** Record failures with an order number and steps to reproduce. Resolve issues that affect money, invoice copies, access or order state before a pilot.
2. **Build cancellation with an advance refund.** Add an Owner action for an unissued order. Show the amount collected, reason and refund method. Save a linked adjustment once, release its table if applicable, and show the refund in Reports and Activity. Test duplicate clicks, partial or multiple payments, stale updates, and cancellation racing with bill issuance. Confirm the desired handling of any retained advance before building that case.
3. **Add account recovery and operational checks.** Add password recovery and session revocation, set up error monitoring, and verify Atlas backup and a restore to a separate database. Confirm invoice adjustment documents with the restaurant's accountant before launch.
4. **Run a small restaurant pilot.** Reconcile pilot bills and refunds with the restaurant's records, check the printer and contact workflow with the actual staff, and agree on rollback steps. Schedule offline billing after the online workflow is stable.
